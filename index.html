02 Process

From idea to ink. Four deliberate stages.

Step 01
1

Consultation

A 45-minute conversation. We map references, placement, scale, story. No deposit until you've met your artist.

Step 02
2

Design

Your artist drafts the piece across two weeks. You see the linework before the needle ever sees you.

Step 03
3

Session

Private suite. Single artist. Four to eight hours. Breaks when you need them. We work until the work is right.

Step 04
4

Aftercare

A full aftercare kit goes home with you. We check in at day three, week two, and a healed-photo follow-up at week six.
