✦ About Alejandro

Single-needle florals and architectural script. Trained in Madrid and Mexico City. Quiet, patient, exact — the long-session whisperer.

Alejandro apprenticed in Madrid under a botanical illustrator before moving to Mexico City. He joined Aristocratic Ink in 2023 as the studio's fine-line lead, working almost exclusively with a single-needle configuration on botanical, architectural and typographic pieces.
