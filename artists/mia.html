✦ About Mia

Painterly washes, illustrative subjects, soft edges. Fine-art background, painter first. Booking by portfolio review.

Mia trained as a watercolor painter at SCAD before apprenticing under a Nashville illustrative tattooer. Her work brings a painter's logic to the medium: blooms of color where you'd expect outlines, soft edges where the skin will let her have them.
