✦ About David

Hyperreal portraits and large-scale compositions. Fourteen years of needle, three continents of guest spots, one obsession with light.

David founded Aristocratic Ink in 2024 after seven years working in private studios across Mexico City, Madrid and Brooklyn. His work moves between photo-real portraiture and large-scale narrative compositions — pieces that read like film stills when you walk past.
